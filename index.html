Equity Valuation of a Semiconductor Manufacturer

Investments · SKKU · Fall 2025

  • Conducted a team-based equity valuation of Samsung Electronics to support an investment recommendation (buy/hold/sell).
  • Built a historical DuPont decomposition of return on equity (ROE) into Net Profit Margin, Asset Turnover, and Equity Multiplier using five years of financial data, applying a sequential year-over-year framework to isolate marginal effects.
  • Demonstrated that over 90% of the 2023 ROE decline was attributable to margin contraction, with asset efficiency and leverage contributing marginally (<1% point each), concluding that Samsung’s profitability is structurally margin driven.
